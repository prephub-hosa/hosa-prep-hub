Study Strategy
High-yield tips for scoring well on this event
  • 01 Lab safety (OSHA, biohazard handling, PPE, spill protocols) is always tested — never skip it.
  • 02 Know normal reference ranges for CBC, BMP, lipid panel — questions often ask what an abnormal value indicates.
  • 03 Urinalysis: normal vs. abnormal findings, what each abnormality suggests clinically.
  • 04 Gram stain: gram positive (purple) vs. gram negative (pink), and example pathogens of each.
  • 05 Quality control in the lab: what systematic vs. random error looks like on a Levey-Jennings chart.
  • 06 Blood typing: ABO/Rh compatibility, universal donor (O-), universal recipient (AB+).
